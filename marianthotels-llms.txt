# Mariant Hotels

> Family-owned hotel group operating three 3- and 4-star hotels on the east coast of Mallorca (Balearic Islands, Spain), in the beach resorts of Sa Coma and S'Illot.

## Hotels

- [Mariant Hotels — Group Overview](https://www.marianthotels.com/en/): Official website of the Mariant Hotels group. Presents all three properties, direct booking advantages, exclusive offers, and the brand's philosophy around Mediterranean hospitality, gastronomy, and sustainability.

- [Hotel Mariant — S'Illot, Mallorca](https://www.marianthotels.com/en/hoteles/hotel-mariant): 3-star family hotel located 150 metres from the beach in S'Illot. Features outdoor pool, buffet restaurant, bar, and air-conditioned rooms with private balcony. Ideal for families.

- [Hotel Mariant — Rooms](https://www.marianthotels.com/en/hoteles/hotel-mariant/habitaciones): Room types available at Hotel Mariant in S'Illot, with details on capacity, amenities, views, and balcony options.

- [Hotel Mariant — How to Get There](https://www.marianthotels.com/en/hoteles/hotel-mariant/como-llegar): Location, address, and directions for Hotel Mariant in S'Illot. Airport transfer information (70 km from Palma de Mallorca Airport).

- [Hotel Mariant Park — Sa Coma, Mallorca](https://www.marianthotels.com/en/hoteles/hotel-mariant-park): 4-star hotel in Sa Coma with full spa and wellness centre, outdoor pools, gardens, and buffet restaurant with show cooking. Suitable for families and couples.

- [Hotel Mariant Park — Rooms](https://www.marianthotels.com/en/hoteles/hotel-mariant-park/habitaciones): Room categories at Hotel Mariant Park. Bright, spacious rooms with sea or garden views, balcony, full bathroom, and modern amenities.

- [Hotel Mariant Park — Services](https://www.marianthotels.com/en/hoteles/hotel-mariant-park/servicios): Full list of services and facilities at Hotel Mariant Park: pools, spa, gym, kids' area, entertainment, restaurant, bar, and car hire desk.

- [Hotel Mariant Park — Gastronomy](https://www.marianthotels.com/en/hoteles/hotel-mariant-park/gastronomia): Culinary offering at Hotel Mariant Park. Buffet restaurant with Mediterranean and international dishes, show cooking stations, themed dinners, and à la carte options.

- [Hotel Mariant Park — FAQs](https://www.marianthotels.com/en/hoteles/hotel-mariant-park/faqs): Frequently asked questions about Hotel Mariant Park: check-in/check-out times, pet policy, parking, accessibility, and booking conditions.

- [Hotel Brisa Marina — S'Illot, Mallorca](https://www.marianthotels.com/en/hoteles/hotel-brisa-marina): 4-star adults-only hotel located between two beaches in S'Illot. Elegant and tranquil environment designed for couples and adults seeking a child-free Mediterranean retreat.

- [Hotel Brisa Marina — Rooms](https://www.marianthotels.com/en/hoteles/hotel-brisa-marina/habitaciones): Room types at Hotel Brisa Marina. Spacious rooms (approx. 30 m²) with sea views, full bathrooms, balcony, and Mediterranean-inspired decor.

## Key Facts

- Hotel group operating since the late 20th century on the east coast of Mallorca, Balearic Islands, Spain
- Three properties: Hotel Mariant (3-star, S'Illot), Hotel Mariant Park (4-star, Sa Coma), Hotel Brisa Marina (4-star adults-only, S'Illot)
- Hotel Brisa Marina is an adults-only property — no children admitted
- Hotel Mariant Park features a full spa with heated pool, Turkish bath, sauna, and body treatments
- Located 150–200 metres from the beach in Sa Coma and S'Illot
- Approximately 70 km from Palma de Mallorca International Airport (PMI)
- Nearby attractions: Caves of Drach, Porto Cristo, Cala Millor, Cala Bona
- Sustainability-focused: solar panels installed, sustainable sourcing policy
- Direct booking available at marianthotels.com with exclusive web rates and immediate confirmation
- Languages: Spanish and English
- Industry: Hospitality / Beach resort hotels / Mediterranean tourism
